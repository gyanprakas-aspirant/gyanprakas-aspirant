## Data analysis with a business purpose

For my upcoming projects, I am developing a structured approach:

1. Clarify the business problem, the stakeholder and the decision to support.
2. Define the KPIs and check data quality before drawing conclusions.
3. Explore patterns and investigate possible causes.
4. Communicate findings, recommendations and limitations clearly.
5. Identify how the impact of a recommendation could be measured.

This connects my operations background with the problem-solving and stakeholder perspective needed in business analysis.
